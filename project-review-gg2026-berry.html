The work began with generic STL files. I adapted and manipulated the geometry to make the forms printable, improving the way the parts could be oriented, sliced, and produced.

  • Challenge: Prepare oversized berry geometry for a desktop 3D printer
  • Solution: Modify the STL files and optimize the parts for efficient printing
  • Large strawberries: Scaled & sliced to fit and avoid overhang
  • Result: Print-ready files for large berry forms
